# 🚲 Cyclistic Case Study  
## Converting Casual Riders into Annual Members

**Author:** Aleksandr Svirskii  
**Role:** Junior Data Analyst Portfolio Project  
**Year:** 2026  

---

## 📌 Business Problem

Cyclistic, a Chicago-based bike-share company, aims to increase annual memberships, as they generate significantly higher long-term profitability compared to casual riders.

This project analyzes behavioral differences between casual riders and annual members to identify data-driven conversion opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Understand how annual members and casual riders use Cyclistic bikes differently in order to support targeted marketing strategies for membership growth.

---

## 📊 Data Source

- 12 months of Divvy bike-share data (public dataset)
- ~5.5 million ride records
- No personally identifiable information (PII) used
- Publicly available under Divvy data license agreement

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- SQL (BigQuery)
- Tableau (data visualization & dashboard design)
- PowerPoint (executive presentation)

---

## 🔎 Data Preparation

- Removed rides with negative durations and rides longer than 24 hours (<0.2% of total data)
- Created `ride_length` variable
- Created `day_of_week` and `hour_of_day` variables
- Standardized columns across monthly datasets
- Merged 12 months into a consolidated dataset
- Aggregated summary metrics for behavioral analysis

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Members Show Commuting Behavior
- Ride peaks at 8 AM and 5 PM
- Strong weekday dominance
- Consistent year-round usage

### 2️⃣ Casual Riders Spend ~60% More Time Per Ride
- Casual average: ~18.6 minutes  
- Member average: ~11.5 minutes  
- Indicates leisure-oriented usage

### 3️⃣ Strong Weekend & Seasonal Pattern for Casual Riders
- Weekend ride concentration
- Significant summer spike
- High engagement during peak periods but limited weekday routine usage

---

## 💡 Strategic Opportunity

Casual riders demonstrate strong engagement but lack consistent weekday commuting behavior.

This signals an opportunity to convert high-frequency seasonal users into recurring annual members.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

- Launch limited-time conversion campaigns targeting high-frequency summer riders
- Introduce seasonal-to-annual upgrade incentives
- Test a weekend-focused subscription tier for recreational users
